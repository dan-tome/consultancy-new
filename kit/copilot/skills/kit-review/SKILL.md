# Kit review

## Step 1. Gather

Look back over this session and anything the user tells you. For each kit skill and rule, note whether it
was used, whether it helped, and anything confusing, too long, or ignored. Also note recurring problems
that no skill covers.

## Step 2. Write

Use exactly this shape, generic and short (under about 30 lines):

```
Kit review:
Period: <e.g. "this session", "past week" as told by the user>
Helped: <skill or rule - one line why>
Didn't help or confusing: <skill or rule - what went wrong>
Not used: <skills never needed, if known>
Missing: <kinds of task with no good skill, in general terms>
Limitations seen: <short list, or "see reports sent">
Suggestions: <changes you think would help>
```

Follow every privacy rule from `kit-ask-consultant` step 1. Describe kinds of task, not their content.

## Step 3. Send

Follow the sending steps of `kit-ask-consultant` (steps 2 to 5: check line by line, show and ask, send,
report).
